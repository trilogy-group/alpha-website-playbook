# Findings from the September 15, 2026 inventory

What the site inventory (M1) and the lifecycle reconciliation (M2) found. Each item is either a decision for Yash and Matt or a defect to file. Nothing here has been changed on the site.

## Lifecycle states as the sources define them

- The growth map API publishes one `programType` per campus with three values: Open (38 programs), Announced (8), Pre-Announcement (31). It comes from the HubSpot Programs object.
- The Operations sheet uses Enrolling, Open, and Pre-Announcement. The observer treats Enrolling as compatible with map Open or Announced.
- `launchDate` is free text on the map program; the only observed value is "Fall 2026".
- No source has Paused, Closed, or Enrolled-with-waitlist. If those states exist in practice, they need a field.

## Conflicts in the sources

1. Seven campuses are `Open` on the map but still carry `launchDate: Fall 2026`: Charlotte, Chicago, Dorado, Palo Alto, Raleigh, Santa Monica, The Woodlands. Either the launch date should be cleared when a campus opens, or Open with a launch date means something. Decision needed.
2. Greenwich: two map programs (Armonk and Port Chester) point at one page, `/greenwich/`. The page copy names Port Chester; the hidden fallback text names Armonk; observer tickets were filed under both names. Decision: one page or two.
3. Lexington: the map program's page is `/boston-suburbs/`; the Locations page links `/lexington/`, which does not exist.
4. East Bay: the map URL lacks a trailing slash (`/east-bay`); every other campus has one.
5. Kirkland's community URL points at `www.community.alpha.school`; La Jolla's has no campus parameter. Both are outside the pattern the other campuses use.

## Page copy that disagrees with the state

- Chicago is Open on the map but the intro still says "is launching in Fall 2026". It was not in the group A batch. Candidate for the same fix once the sentence rule is approved.
- Charlotte is Open and reads "is open K-8 for Fall 2026", a third wording.
- Franklin, San Juan, and Houston Heights are Announced and read "is coming soon" with no month or year, which the requirements catalog already treats as a defect.
- Bethesda is Announced and reads "opens January 2027", the only page that follows the named-date pattern.

## Intro card media

- 14 Open campuses have an "Open and Enrolling" card, 15 have a video, 13 still have older images including "Coming Fall 2026" graphics. Atlanta, La Jolla, and Santa Monica are the Open campuses with a stale card and no approved replacement. Yash makes the cards from one template.

## Facts across surfaces

From the campus facts check on September 8: Austin's Locations card says PK-8 and the page says PreK-5; Houston's card tuition is $40,000 and the page says $50,000; Bethesda's card says $65,000 and the page says $50,000; San Juan and Franklin cards have no tuition. Neither surface is authoritative on its own, so each pair needs a source ruling.

## Template variants

Austin and Miami use an older template; Kirkland has three unnamed trailing sections; Chicago and Greenwich have news sections; section order varies on 20 pages. Decision: which variants are allowed, and whether Austin and Miami should be migrated to the current template.
