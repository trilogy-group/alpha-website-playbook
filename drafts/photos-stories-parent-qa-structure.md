# Photos & stories → parent Q&A (per campus)

Use this when campus website tickets bring photos or stories. National questions stay the same. Local answers and assets change by campus.

Status: draft for ops use. Playbook later when the pattern is boring.

## How to prepare (right way)

1. Keep one **national question bank** (below). Do not invent a new FAQ taxonomy per city.
2. For each campus, fill a **campus card** (template). Only local proof, local faces, local events, local objections that show up in that market.
3. When an APEX Website ticket lands with photos/stories, run the **ticket triage prompt**. Execute the page change, then map the asset to a question/objection if it fits.
4. FAQs should not be identical everywhere. Same questions, different local proof.
5. Quality bar: full rooms, smiling kids, kids+adults interacting. Reject "proof of life" phone snaps for public pages (Haven / MacKenzie).
6. Brand: MacKenzie SoT for voice. No em dashes. Hide weak surfaces rather than ship bad UX.

## National question bank (parent ask → what content must show)

| ID | Parent question | Objection / fear underneath | What a photo or story must prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q01 | Will my kid have real friends / peers? | "This is glorified homeschool" | Groups of kids learning, playing, arriving, collaborating. Prefer wide group shots over lone portraits. |
| Q02 | What does a normal day look like? | Abstract marketing, no concrete day | Limitless Launch, learning blocks, workshops, kids coaching adults, a clear "aha" moment. |
| Q03 | How much screen time is there? | Devices = babysitting | Apps in context with learning science, not kids zoned out on tablets. Pair with Time Back story. |
| Q04 | What is Time Back, really? | "Just a software pitch" | Academic results first, then AlphaPhonics / Read / Write, brief in-app, learning scientists. Not a product tour. |
| Q05 | How do you build behavior, independence, life skills? | Soft skills = fluff | Concrete activities: games, ropes, camping, workshops, teamwork. Tie to Me / We / Key or Core skills when approved. |
| Q06 | What do I do when my child struggles? | Swoop-in parenting vs Alpha model | Parent tenets: support don't swoop. Clear language, not vibes. |
| Q07 | Why a Dean of Parents instead of the guide? | "I can't reach the teacher" | DOP as parent educator for Alpha's model; answers parent questions so guides stay with kids. |
| Q08 | Are the guides any good? What if they leave? | Guide rotation anxiety | Credential-forward guide stories; mobility as ambition + strong successors, not abandonment. |
| Q09 | Is Alpha right for our family? | Wrong-fit enrollments | Fit priorities (academics, life skills, campus feel). Local answers can differ by city. |
| Q10 | Will academics hold up (esp. high school)? | "Life skills school, weak academics" | High school outcomes, AP/SAT proof, college paths when we have real numbers. |
| Q11 | What is World School? | Volunteerism optics / gimmick | Student journey and agency. Avoid savior / volunteerism framing in photos. |
| Q12 | Can I trust leadership and the parent community? | Founder drama, cold school | Real local parents + leadership together; local community, not generic "community" copy. |

Add campus-only questions under the campus card (e.g. dual-campus choice, parking, facility). Do not dilute the national twelve.

## Ticket triage prompt (paste into agent / operator checklist)

You are handling an Alpha campus Website ticket that includes photos or stories.

Inputs: APEX ticket, campus slug, assets, requester ask, live page URL.

Do this in order:
1. Execute only if APEX has URL + change + assets. Else Missing Info to campus DoP/DoA.
2. Name the best-fit national Q ID(s) from the bank (or "none / one-off").
3. Name the parent objection it addresses in one plain sentence.
4. Decide destination: campus page section, Meet our Team, Resources/FAQ, community app spotlight, or execute-only (no FAQ).
5. Quality gate: reject for public use if proof-of-life, no smiles, empty room, unclear subject, stock-looking, or AI-slop caption. Prefer kids+adults interacting and full rooms.
6. Brand gate: MacKenzie voice; no em dashes; no unsupported claims; no identical FAQ copy copied from another campus without local proof.
7. If HubSpot-synced fact (grades, address, tuition, apply link): do not invent from a story. HubSpot first.
8. Output for the ticket comment:
   - What we published (live URL)
   - Q ID + objection
   - Destination
   - Whether this should update the campus card / FAQ (yes/no + why)

Never invent parent concerns that admissions did not surface. Prefer real ticket and DoA language.
