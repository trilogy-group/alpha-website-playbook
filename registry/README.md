# Registry

- `n8n-workflows.json`: reviewed October 7 metadata for 17 selected workflows/dependencies, active versus saved versions, schedules, credential types and current publishing observations. Raw exports remain private. This is an observed inventory, not permission to execute or a complete organization inventory.

- `sources.json`: where every fact and cross-system identity comes from, with access notes and authority boundaries.
  `n8n.sharedProgramReader` records the October 7 reader/HubDB contract, accessible consumer inventory and ownership gap; use `spec/program-data-reliability.json` for acceptance checks. Its observations do not certify every integration or install a monitor.
- `campus-pages.json`: campus slug to URL, WordPress object, map program ids, and the names tickets use.
- `campus-identities.json`: partial, verified HubSpot program ID crosswalk to Community IDs, slugs, visibility, and canonical Community destinations. A missing row must not be inferred.
- `campus-open-media.json`: approved open-campus intro media per campus and who produces it; `template` is the one master the Open and Enrolling cards are generated from (`agent/generate-open-card.mjs`, `protocols/recipes/open-card.md`), `source` null until Yash Chitneni supplies it (`template.ask`). A row is the approval only: `openMedia` (card, video or null), `attachmentId`, `status`, and where present `owner`, `state`, `evidence`, `addedBy`. Never a description of what the slot shows today (the `current` and `apexStatus` notes were removed 2026-09-28 after a note written on 2026-09-15 outlived the asset it described and reached the site truth report): the observer packet describes the slot and `tests/site-truth-report.mjs` grades it from that observation.
- `campus-leadership.json`: per campus, the leadership shown on the page (Head of Schools, Dean of Parents, Director of Admissions, guides), plus placeholder cards found.
  `groups` (1.1, 2026-09-24): campus groups with shared leadership (first: Greater Boston, boston, jamaica-plain, boston-suburbs); the identity check rows read it through `exemptionsRef`, so a shared leader's card naming a sibling campus is not an other-campus conflict.
- `campus-cta-exceptions.json`: named, dated, expiring exceptions to the campus CTA check rows, keyed by campus slug and CTA (`book-a-call`, `calendar`, `packet`, `apply`, `community`), each with rule (`hidden`, `present`, `destination`), reason, decidedBy, source (channel and date), decidedOn, expires (ISO date, `lifecycle:Open`, or null) and note. Schema `spec/schemas/campus-cta-exceptions.schema.json`; the filer reads it through the rows' `exemptionsRef` and reports a matching finding as `exempted`; entries are written by `agent/record-decision.mjs` together with the `docs/decisions.md` row (first entries: Lexington Book a Call hidden while Announced; Dorado Book a Call destination, moved from `spec/campus-page-controls.json`).
- `website-measurement-contract.json`: Website as a Funnel layer 3. Version 1.0 records Matt as interim analytics owner; approved completion meanings for all five journeys; 30-day first-touch and latest-meaningful-touch views; raw-plus-screened reporting; discovery of the real Book a Call and Info Session destinations; privacy-safe downstream identity candidates; the live governed `dk1` destination-identity parameter in GTM version 7; and the event-scoped GA4 `destination_key` registration. `destination_key` remains diagnostic until processed population and controlled authority reconciliation pass; it is not a completion join or key event. Consent ownership, primary destination instrumentation, verified completion joins, screening thresholds, and key-event promotion remain explicit gaps an agent never fills by inference. Its `healthConfig` is the rules source for the AEO measurement-integrity workflow.
- `people.json`: people and the roles they hold, each with a source and date. People change; `spec/responsibilities.json` holds the roles.
  `formerRoles`, when present, retains historical assignments and the source of their withdrawal; it is not an active routing source.
- `observer-checks.json`: the production observer's check catalog.
- `missing-info-routing.json` / `missing-info-routing.md`: Missing Info class to role routing (people never in rules; resolve via people.json or live campus resolver).
- `campus-roles-snapshot.json` / `campus-roles-overrides.json`: live campus-role cache and Hailey-confirmed overrides.
