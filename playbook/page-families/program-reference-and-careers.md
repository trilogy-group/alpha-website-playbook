## Requirements

Common fields from `spec/page-family-requirements.json` apply. In addition:

- Each program page explains one part of the model in parent language and links to the canonical Alpha Answers entry for the objection it resolves. It does not restate statistics that live elsewhere; it links to them.
- Every statistic, ranking, or outcome claim on these pages is in the claim registry with its source and boundary (national, class-level, campus-level). Held claims wait for MacKenzie (Q-07).
- `/faq/` is the single FAQ; campus pages and posts may embed a subset but do not fork the answers. FAQPage schema is emitted once per page, not duplicated on unrelated posts (the survey found the 84-item FAQ schema injected on a blog post).
- `/guides/` and any guide-proof surface follow the guide proof model once decided (Q-08). Until then no new guide directory pages are created.
- `/founders/` and author pages carry approved biographies only; author pages are noindex unless website operations decides otherwise (Q-22).
- `/video-library/` and `/virtual-tour/` reference approved media from the media registry; embedded YouTube players must not autoplay with sound.
- `/resources-<city>/` pages are campus-scoped reference pages. Each must match its campus identity (name, facts, links) the same way a campus page does, or be redirected to the campus page. Whether they should exist at all is Q-23.
- `/careers/` either recruits (owner, form, ATS link, and approval recorded) or links out to the hiring system. Today its owner and purpose are unrecorded (Q-24).
- Title tags follow one pattern per sub-family. The survey found `/admission/` titled "Private School in Austin" and other pattern drift; the pattern itself is Q-25.
