# Audience and Messaging

Status: Proposed for adoption by MacKenzie Price (brand authority) and Yash Chitneni (website operations). The profile and messaging spine below were written in two August 2026 review memos in Matt Barge's marketing workspace (`docs/migration/refresh-icp-messaging-form-factor-review-2026-08-26.md`, `docs/migration/alpha-website-reimagining-criteria-2026-08-28.md`). Those memos evaluated a redesign candidate and disclaimed authority over the live site. This file promotes their audience and messaging content to a standing standard for alpha.school, pending approval. Word-level rules stay in `playbook/site/site-rules.md` and MacKenzie's recorded choices; this file does not restate them.

## 1. Primary audience

A parent actively evaluating a private-school alternative for a school-age child. They are deciding whether Alpha could be a better life for their kid, not browsing education theory.

| Role | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer | Parent or parent household |
| Payer | Parent or household, with ESA or voucher context in some states (Q-05: which states and how the site may mention it) |
| User | The child |
| Influencers | The child, co-parent, current Alpha parents, local parent networks, press, search and AI answers, the local campus team, state and private-school approval signals |

Tensions the parent arrives with (one or more):

- The child is bored, under-challenged, unhappy, or moving too slowly.
- The child is high-performing and not being stretched.
- The child is bright but disengaged from school.
- They want rigor without giving up happiness, friends, confidence, or real-world growth.
- They like innovation but worry about screens, AI, socialization, legitimacy, and adult support.
- They are comparing Alpha with elite private schools or strong local options.

Trigger events:

- Heard about Alpha from another parent, an article, a podcast, an event, or a video.
- A move, a new school year, a school transition, or dissatisfaction with the current school.
- Saw a student transformation story.
- Searched "best private school in [city]" or "Alpha School [city] reviews".
- Considering a new Alpha market and needs to know the campus is real, approved, and credible.

## 2. Who the site is not for

Enrolled families. Their surfaces are the Community app, TimeBack, and until the 2026-27 cutover ParentSquare (Confirmed: Brainlift Insight 6; Tasha Arnold, `#supportcollab`, 2026-07-29). Nothing on alpha.school should assume the reader is already a customer. Where enrolled families need admissions forms or re-enrollment, Q-06 asks whether those paths belong here or on the Community app.

Secondary audiences (press, job seekers, city champions, regulators) are listed in `website-charter.md` section 3 with the question of whether each is served or merely used as proof (Q-02).

## 3. The belief sequence

Every page family should move the parent along this sequence. The homepage carries all seven steps in miniature; other families carry one or two deeply.

1. Alpha is not a normal private school.
2. Kids love school here.
3. Kids learn twice as much in two hours.
4. The rest of the day is human, social, challenging, and real-world.
5. The adults are exceptional guides, not generic teachers.
6. The proof is credible: real students, real families, real outcomes, public and third-party validation.
7. The next step is concrete and local.

## 4. Questions the site must answer

A parent who leaves without an answer to any of these has been failed by the site. Each answer should live on one canonical page that other pages link to (the Alpha Answers family exists for this).

| Question | Canonical home today (Observed) | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Will my kid love school here? | Homepage, campus parent quotes | Localized quotes on 13 of 44 campus pages; 31 use a generic block |
| Will my kid be socially connected, known, and supported? | `/the-program/`, `/lifeskills-workshops/` | No single answer page |
| Are the academic claims real? | `/faq/`, blog, `/end-of-2025-parent-survey-results/` | Approved statistics and exact wording need verification (Q-07) |
| What does "learn 2x in 2 hours" actually mean? | `/2-hour-learning/` | |
| Is this just kids on computers? | `/blog/does-alpha-school-replace-teachers-with-ai/`, `/ai/` posts | |
| What do guides do, and why trust them? | Campus Team sections, `/guides/` | The ICP review rejected the directory model for the homepage; the campus Team section is still a directory (Q-08) |
| What does the rest of the day look like? | `/lifeskills-workshops/`, `/workshops/`, video resources | |

| What should I do next? | Campus CTAs, `/admission/`, events | Primary next step undecided (Q-01) |

## 5. Messaging spine

The three commitments are the site's narrative architecture. Everything else supports, proves, localizes, or makes them vivid.

1. Kids love school.
2. Kids learn 2x in 2 hours.
3. Kids learn life skills.

Preferred and avoided public language is governed by `playbook/site/site-rules.md` (MacKenzie's recorded word-level choices, including "twice as much, never twice as fast" and "love school, never love learning"). Where this file and that one differ, that one wins.

Terminology that must be verified with MacKenzie before it appears in new copy (Q-07):

- Official spelling and capitalization of TimeBack and Alpha Experience. Answered in `playbook/site/mackenzie-style-choices.md`: AlphaTest (formerly Test2Pass), Q Breaks (not Queue Breaks), Shadow Day.
- Whether Shadow Day, Shadow Week, or event-first language is the approved public next step.
- Current approved statistics and exact wording for the 2x and top 1% claims.
- Whether "programs" or "campuses" is the public noun for the network count.

## 6. Proof standard

Only Alpha's best proof is elevated. Hierarchy, strongest first:

1. A real student's transformation in the student's own words.
2. A real parent's story with name, child's grade or age, and city, where permission exists.
3. Before-and-after academic or behavior data with methodology.
4. External validation: press, approvals, credible third parties.
5. Guide and team credentials, curated around parent trust rather than as a directory.
6. Broader statistics, only with a plain-language interpretation.

Every major claim answers four questions: what exactly is claimed, what source proves it, whether it is national, local, class-level, or student-level, and whether the visible copy stays inside that boundary. A quote needs a permission record (Q-10: where permission records live and who keeps them).

## 7. Tone

Confident, direct, specific, ambitious, parent-literate, proof-first, future-facing. Not corporate, not school brochure, not cute for its own sake. Make Alpha feel exceptional without sounding like generic private-school marketing.

## 8. Message role by page family

Proposed. Each family owns a stage of the sequence and one dominant next step. Two families at different stages should not share a primary CTA (Brainlift Insight 8).

| Family | Owns belief steps | Message job | Dominant next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | 1 through 7 in miniature | Startle, promise, prove, route to a city | Q-01 (event-first is the ICP review's recommendation) |
| Program pages (`/the-program/`, `/2-hour-learning/`, workshops) | 3, 4, 5 | Explain the model in parent language | Route to Locations |
| Alpha Answers (`/ai/`), blog, FAQ | 6, plus whichever objection the page resolves | Canonical, crawlable answers with sources | Route to the relevant campus or event |
| News, podcasts, founders, video resources | 6 | Third-party and founder credibility | Route to Discover pages, not straight to Apply |
| Locations and map | 7 | Find the right campus and its state | View campus, or Community for pre-announcement |
| Campus page, Open | 2, 5, 6, 7 localized | Local proof, local team, local next step | Book a Call or Apply per HubSpot Program values |
| Campus page, Announced | 1, 7 | Real, approved, coming; capture interest | Waitlist or Community |
| Campus page, Pre-announcement | 7 | Route only | Community destination |
| Admissions and forms | 7 | Remove friction, state what happens next | Apply or Book a Call |
| Confirmation pages | none | Confirm the action and set expectations | The next operational step, nothing else |
| Legal | none | Accurate, current, unparaphrased | none |
| Careers, Bring Alpha to Your City, campaign landings | separate audiences | Q-02 | per campaign owner |

## 9. What this file does not decide

- The primary homepage CTA and the operational offer behind it (info session, Alpha Experience, Shadow Day, or other). Q-01.
- The guide proof model for the campus Team section versus the homepage. Q-08.
- Approved statistics, quotes, and video clips. Q-07, Q-10.
- Whether a parent should be asked their city first, have it inferred, or see the national story first. Q-11.
